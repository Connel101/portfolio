About me

Engineering useful answers from difficult problems.

I am a software developer who enjoys turning fragmented data and manual processes into dependable tools people can use.

My work spans scalable backend systems, business applications, data acquisition, OCR, enterprise automation, and advanced search. I work mainly with Python, Django, Flask, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, and Elasticsearch.

I am particularly interested in the point where software meets a real operational constraint: legacy systems, unstructured documents, complex datasets, or workflows that need to become faster and more reliable.
